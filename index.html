VERDICT Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

VERDICT is an application designed to allow users to create a profile, meet other users and cast a Like or Dislike vote following an interaction. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using VERDICT.

Information We Process

Depending on how the application is used, VERDICT may process information such as a user's chosen first and last name, profile photo, application/device identifiers, voting information, QR codes and temporary verification codes used to facilitate interactions between users.

Profile Photos

Users may voluntarily select a profile photo from their device. The application accesses the selected image only when the user chooses to add or change their profile photo.

QR Codes and Voting

VERDICT can generate temporary QR codes and verification codes to allow users who meet to establish an interaction and vote. These codes are temporary and may expire automatically.

Purpose of Data Processing

Information is processed only as necessary to provide and protect the features of VERDICT, including user profiles, interaction verification, voting, abuse prevention, security and application functionality.

Data Sharing

VERDICT does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers when necessary to operate, secure and maintain the application, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by VERDICT. However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the operation, security and legitimate purposes of the application, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their use of VERDICT by contacting the developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.

Children

VERDICT is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If such information is identified, appropriate steps will be taken to remove it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when VERDICT's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
