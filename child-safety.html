VERDICT Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 25, 2026

VERDICT has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). VERDICT is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

Users must not create, upload, share, request, promote, facilitate, or distribute content involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children, including grooming, sextortion, trafficking, sexual endangerment of minors, or CSAM.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users may report safety concerns or inappropriate activity through VERDICT's available reporting and contact mechanisms. Reports involving suspected CSAE or CSAM are treated as high priority.

Enforcement

VERDICT may remove prohibited content, restrict or terminate access, preserve information when legally required, and take other appropriate actions when violations of these standards are identified.

Reporting to Authorities

VERDICT will comply with applicable laws concerning child safety. Where legally required, suspected or confirmed CSAM or child sexual exploitation will be reported to the appropriate authorities or designated child-safety reporting organizations.
